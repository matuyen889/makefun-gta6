An original 3D sun-drenched coastal open-world crime action game built for desktop browsers (offline, zero external requests, pure ES modules + WebGL). Set across the fictional subtropical metropolis of **Caleta del Sol** across 4 vibrant districts: Malecón del Sol, Distrito Nocturno, Dársena Marina, and Muelle Pesquero.

## Key Features

- **Living 3D City & Atmosphere:**
  - High-performance WebGL 3D rendering with procedural coastal skyline, beachfront hotels, palm trees, neon marquees, dynamic shadows, and sunset golden-hour lighting.
  - Full pedestrian population with civilian AI, wandering behavior, ambient chatter, and panic/ragdoll physics.
  - Dynamic street traffic across two-way boulevards with vehicle pathfinding, headlights, collisions, and carjacking mechanics.
  - In-vehicle radio stations (Sol FM 94.2 Latin House, Vice Wave 101.5 Synthwave, Radio Caleta 88.9).

- **Vehicles & Driving Physics:**
  - Multiple distinct vehicles: *Tormenta GT* (high-speed sports coupe), *Vaquero 440* (classic muscle), *Solstice V12* (exotic supercar), and *MSPD Interceptor* (pursuit cruiser).
  - Responsive acceleration, steering, drift braking, suspension roll, skid marks, particle smoke, and impact deformation.

- **On-Foot Combat & Weapon Arsenal:**
  - Smooth third-person movement with camera rotation, sprint, tactical roll, crosshair targeting, and weapon wheel.
  - 5-tier arsenal: Fists, Heavy .45 Pistol, Viper 9mm SMG, Breacher 12G Shotgun, and Carbine AR-15 with recoil, muzzle flashes, and ballistic tracer effects.

- **5-Star Tactical Police Response (MSPD):**
  - **1 Star:** Foot patrol alert & inquiry.
  - **2 Stars:** Squad car pursuit units responding with sirens and PIT maneuvers.
  - **3 Stars:** Strategic roadblock checkpoints and tire spike strips.
  - **4 Stars:** Tactical armored SWAT vans and flank teams.
  - **5 Stars:** CONDOR-1 Tactical Air Patrol helicopter with active spotlight, rotor wash, and marksman support.
  - Dynamic Wanted HUD with active unit distance ticker and radar cones.

- **8-Part Story Operation Campaign:**
  1. *Operation 1: Breakout & Sunbreak* — High-speed courier car heist on the coastal highway.
  2. *Operation 2: Docks Infiltration* — Infiltrate rival contraband depots at Muelle Pesquero.
  3. *Operation 3: Neon Bazaar Ambush* — Intense market shootout against Los Cuervos syndicate.
  4. *Operation 4: Oceanview Vault* — Safecracking operation with thermal drill.
  5. *Operation 5: Highway Intercept* — Ram and disable the armored convoy.
  6. *Operation 6: Marina Infiltration* — High-speed speedboat assault on the cartel yacht.
  7. *Operation 7: 5-Star Siege* — Evade full MSPD SWAT lockdown and Condor-1 air searchlights.
  8. *Operation 8: Sunset Syndicate* — Climax penthouse confrontation at dusk.

- **Comprehensive GTA HUD, Map & Progression:**
  - Real-time rotating minimap with player blip, police line-of-sight cones, mission checkpoints, spray shops, hospitals, and safehouses.
  - Pause menu with interactive district map, 100% completion checklist, career statistics, radio tuner, and 3 persistent save slots.

## Controls

- `W / A / S / D` or `Arrow Keys`: Move / Steer vehicle
- `Shift`: Sprint / Nitro Boost
- `Space`: Handbrake / Jump / Tactical Roll
- `F` or `Enter`: Enter / Exit vehicle (Carjack)
- `Mouse Movement`: Aim camera / Crosshair
- `Left Click`: Attack / Fire weapon
- `Right Click`: Aim down sights
- `1 - 5` or `Scroll`: Select weapon
- `Z / X / C`: Crew tactical support (Swap Plates, Quick Fix, Radar Sweep)
- `R`: Cycle radio stations
- `Tab / M / Esc`: Open World Map & Career Stats / Pause
